## Geographic support

- [Antioquia government tourism inventory](https://www.antioquia.gov.co/images/pdf/anuario_2012/data/turismo/1622.html) places El Retiro in Oriente and lists La Fe/Los Salados and San Sebastián La Castellana. Use this older inventory for stable geography only.
- [Colombia's official tourism site](https://colombia.travel/es/medellin/conoce-la-reserva-ecologica-san-sebastian-la-castellana) locates the ecological reserve in El Retiro and describes its natural setting.
- [Municipal rural tourism map](https://retiroantioquia.micolombiadigital.gov.co/sites/retiroantioquia/content/files/000064/3184_mapas-el-retiro-turismo-2019-z-rural.pdf) provides further rural context.
