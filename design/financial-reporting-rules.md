# Financial reporting rules

These are the user's defaults for financial statements in this presentation and its English and Spanish versions.

- Leave zero-valued numeric cells visually blank. Keep numeric zeros in source data and calculations. Values that round to zero at the displayed precision are also blank; missing values and formula errors must not be treated as zeros. Preserve rows and columns so statements remain comparable.
- Put percentages directly below key profit results, including contribution margin, EBITDA, net income and the derived result after carry. State the denominator. Aggregate margins divide aggregate profit by aggregate revenue, never average annual percentages.
- Retain the workbook's operating income and net income. Keep net income after carry and its margin visible in the income statement, with a collapsed carry-deduction row that expands on click. Use the modeled allocation timing, and distinguish that profit result from cash distributions including returned capital.
- Include income statement, after-carry results, statement of cash flows and balance sheet. Follow source classifications, reconcile cash-flow sections and beginning-to-ending cash, and tie ending cash to the balance sheet.
- Sum flows over time. Do not sum beginning or ending cash balances: the lifetime column uses the first opening and last closing balance.
- Keep unrounded source values, formulas, sheet references and source hash. Display USD in whole dollars, negative values in parentheses, and profit margins to one decimal. Disclose model projections and rounding. No source workbook edits or recalculation are implied by website reporting work.
- Keep Excel filenames, workbook versions, sheet/cell references, source details and reconciliation commentary in internal project records. Investor pages show the statements with concise units, periods, margin basis and projection status. Do not add financial tutorials or lengthy explanations.

The same rules apply to local simulations, generated site pages and reusable reporting skills. Publication remains a separate task from a local preview update.
