Private offering

Units in Dulcinea Investments, LLC are intended to be offered in the United States only to accredited investors under Rule 501(a) of Regulation D, in reliance on Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act of 1933 and Rule 506(b). That exemption does not permit general solicitation or advertising. Any offer is made only through definitive offering documents after investor qualification, and only where lawful. Access to this presentation does not establish investor eligibility.

Unregistered units and transfers

The Units are unregistered, restricted securities. Resale requires registration or an applicable exemption. Transfers are also subject to the Operating Agreement, including Supermajority Approval unless a permitted-transfer exception applies, and securities-law and KYC/AML requirements. Neither the SEC nor any state securities regulator has approved the Units or assessed this presentation’s accuracy or adequacy.

Issuer and management

Dulcinea Investments, LLC is a Delaware limited liability company formed on February 4, 2026, and the issuer of the investment presented as Dulcinea One. Its Managing Member is KIT Capital LLC, whose Managing Director is K. Dov Isaza Tuzman. Dulcinea is a real estate investment firm; it oversees its own investments and does not offer brokerage or third-party property-management services.

Colombian structure

Colombian properties are intended to be held through Dulcinea’s Colombian branch, subject to applicable registration and title-transfer requirements. Properties described as signed or in negotiation may not close, or may close on different terms.

Fees and conflicts

Under the Operating Agreement, KIT Capital LLC receives 20% carried interest, calculated property by property after Members recover their contributed capital. No separate asset-management, acquisition or disposition fee is payable to the Managing Member unless approved by Supermajority Approval. Acquisition, hospitality and other service arrangements may involve separate compensation and potential conflicts of interest, including where management or related parties have interests in service providers. The definitive documents and applicable agreements govern.

Capital at risk

An investment in Dulcinea One is speculative and illiquid, with a risk of partial or total loss and no public market for the Units. Property values, rental demand, renovation costs, COP/USD exchange rates, Colombian law and tax, and the timing of sales may affect results. Returns, distributions and exit dates are not guaranteed.
