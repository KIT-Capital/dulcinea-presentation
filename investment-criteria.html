Acquisition criteria

The homes we choose. The discipline behind each acquisition.

Ten acquisition tests

KIT Capital applies these criteria to properties selected for Dulcinea One.

  1. 01

    Location

    El Poblado & El Oriente

    Apartments and penthouses in El Poblado; houses on land in El Oriente.

  2. 02

    Enjoyment

    Homes we’d want to use

    Places investors would want to spend time in, alongside the investment return.

  3. 03

    Product

    300–600 m²

    Distinctive, larger homes with room to add value.

  4. 04

    Entry price

    20–30% below improved value

    Compare all-in cost with the home’s renovated comparable value.

  5. 05

    Transformation

    About 6 months

    A full remodel, including furniture, fixtures and equipment, to a defined budget.

  6. 06

    Due diligence

    Technical. Legal. Title.

    Full review before purchase, with a works budget and contingency.

  7. 07

    Operation

    $300–$1,000 per night

    Paid guest stays have a 28-night minimum and a target cap of US$9,500 per 28 nights.

  8. 08

    Hold & exit

    24–36 months

    From purchase to sale, after renovation and a period of rental operation.

  9. 09

    Deal size

    $750K–$2M

    All-in cost per home.

  10. 10

    Minimum return

    12% investor IRR

    Investor return target, after carry and gains tax.

Criteria marked as met reflect estimates of anticipated results for each property and the portfolio as a whole. They are subject to underwriting and due diligence, not confirmation of achieved results or a guarantee of future performance.

What full due diligence covers

Structure, roof, waterproofing, plumbing, electrical systems, humidity, site and soil; permits and licensing; title and liens; HOA rental rules and pending assessments. A works budget with contingency is required before commitment.

Return targets and assumptions

The minimum target is a 12% investor IRR after carry and gains tax. The current portfolio projects 14.6%; returns are not guaranteed.

The nightly range and deal-size range are screening targets. Rental rates and budgets are underwritten property by property and may fall outside these ranges.

Enjoyment is a property-selection objective. Any investor use rights, allocation or access are governed by the Membership documents.

Selection targets. Membership documents govern.
