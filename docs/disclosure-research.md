## Research and editorial choices

- [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/pro%20forma) and the [SEC Financial Reporting Manual, Topic 3](https://www.sec.gov/about/divisions-offices/division-corporation-finance/financial-reporting-manual/frm-topic-3) use **pro forma**, two words. These pages remain explicitly labeled unaudited projections; the label does not assert compliance with Regulation S-X Article 11 or turn forecasts into historical results.
- The [SEC investor bulletin on private placements](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/private-placements-under-regulation-d-updated-investor-bulletin) supports emphasizing illiquidity, possible total loss, material information and governing offering documents. The term sheet refers to Rule 506(b) and accredited investors; the website notice does not assert that an exemption's conditions have been met or that a shared-password gate establishes compliance.
- The [FTC's disclosure guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2014/09/full-disclosure) emphasizes readability, prominence, contrast and proximity rather than a universal minimum font size. It is general advertising guidance, not a securities-specific compliance opinion.
- [Fundrise's homepage](https://fundrise.com/) uses a compact disclosure with a link to its [full notice](https://fundrise.com/legal/disclosure). The inspected homepage disclosure computed to 12px with 18px line height. Dulcinea uses 12px readable homepage/criteria disclosure text, solid backgrounds and dark type; the full Disclaimer page uses 14px body text. This is a design benchmark, not a legally approved font size.

Homepage risk language is visible without opening a disclosure control. Financial projections and criteria have adjacent qualifications; the navigation also links to the full notice. English and Spanish have equivalent wording. The login screen is unchanged.
