Research provides terminology, not additional Dulcinea rights:

- [ILPA Principles definition](https://ilpa.org/wp-content/flash/ILPA%20Principles%203.0/inc/html/41.html): carried interest is the agreed share of investment profits accruing to the GP.
- [CFA Institute explanation](https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/professional-learning/refresher-readings/2026/general-partner-investor-perspectives-and-investment-process): fund managers receive performance-based compensation in the form of carried interest.
- [Blue Owl GP stakes guide](https://wealth.blueowl.com/solutions/investment-strategy-gp-strategic-capital-gp-stakes): broader GP stakes may include management fees, balance-sheet returns and carried interest. Those broader rights are not assumed here.
