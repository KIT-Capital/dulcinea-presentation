Stays and member rates

A clean, maintained home
The fund covers the home and housekeeping between owner stays. The cost of owner use, including rental income given up, is already reflected in the financial projections.
Optional services at cost
Chef, concierge and other luxury services are available at cost and billed separately to the member.
33% off other Lola & Ber stays
Member rates apply to stays at other Lola & Ber properties, subject to availability and booking terms.
25% off paid stays for family and friends
Family and friends receive the discount on paid stays booked by a member. This is separate from using your allocated owner nights.

Shared participation

3% collective stake in Lola & Ber
At full subscription, members collectively receive 3% of the Lola & Ber hospitality brand business under the membership terms. It is a shared allocation, not 3% per member.
3% of future-fund carry
Dulcinea One members collectively share 3% of the carry pool in future Dulcinea funds, allocated pro rata to their commitments. Carry is the manager’s share of performance profits. It is separate from returns on a member’s own investment in a future fund.
Priority to invest in Dulcinea II
Members have a right of first refusal to invest in Dulcinea II, pro rata to their Dulcinea One commitment and subject to the offering terms.

Future funds may not be raised, and profits or distributions are not guaranteed. Brand equity and future-fund participation are excluded from the projected Dulcinea One return. Membership documents govern these rights.

How booking works

Book any home in service, within your allocation and the available dates. A member stay never displaces a confirmed paying guest. Peak and special dates follow separate allocation rules.

Plan ahead or use open dates

Advance stays can be requested 31 days to 24 months ahead. Short-notice stays use open dates 2–30 days ahead. The manager coordinates placement around existing guest bookings.

Stay length and swaps

The minimum owner stay is two nights, or three nights on special dates. Members may swap confirmed stays with each other, with confirmation within 48 hours.

When plans change

Contact the manager and cancel as soon as your plans change so the dates can reopen for others. If you do not use a booked stay that has not been cancelled, notify the manager at least 48 hours before the scheduled departure; otherwise, cleaning fees will be charged.

Family, friends and house rules

You may invite family and friends and remain responsible for their stay. Owner nights cannot be sold or sublet. Occupancy limits, check-in and check-out times, house rules and responsibility for damage apply.
