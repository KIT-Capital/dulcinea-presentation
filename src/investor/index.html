How booking works↗
Owner use and booking+

Member benefits

Owner use

Members book from the annual pool as each home opens.

365shared nights a year, with five active homes
73per home in service
~5 nightsper US$100,000 at full subscription
12 monthsahead for peak bookings

Any home, any open date

Book any home in service for at least two nights. Swaps within 48 hours. Cancel 30 days ahead.

Peak months

December, January, July and August open for booking 12 months ahead. Bookings close 6 months ahead. Each Member is limited to a quarter of a home’s peak nights.

Special dates

Christmas to New Year, Semana Santa and Feria de las Flores: one stay per home, three nights minimum. A draw weighted by Units allocates stays. Winners sit out the next draw.

Guests come first

An owner stay never displaces a paying guest. Owner nights cost the fund about $142K in rent over its life, already included in the base case.

Nights are allocated pro rata to commitment: about 52 per $1M at full subscription. Membership documents govern availability and all rights.

How Dulcinea operates

Dulcinea is a real estate investment firm focused on Medellín, Colombia.

Dulcinea One is our first fund. We acquire, improve, operate and sell the homes. You invest in the portfolio.
