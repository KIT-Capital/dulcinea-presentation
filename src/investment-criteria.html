Investment criteria

The homes we choose. The discipline behind each acquisition.

Ten acquisition tests

KIT Capital’s Buy Box™ guides property selection for the Dulcinea One fund.

  1. 01

    Location

    El Poblado & El Retiro

    Apartments and penthouses in El Poblado; houses on land in El Retiro.

  2. 02

    Enjoyment

    Homes we’d want to use

    Places investors would want to spend time in, alongside the investment return.

  3. 03

    Product

    300–600 m²

    Distinctive, larger homes with room to add value.

  4. 04

    Entry price

    20–30% below improved value

    Compare all-in cost with the home’s renovated comparable value.

  5. 05

    Transformation

    About 6 months

    A full remodel, including furniture, fixtures and equipment, to a defined budget.

  6. 06

    Due diligence

    Technical. Legal. Title.

    Full review before purchase, with a works budget and contingency.

  7. 07

    Operation

    $300–$1,000 per night

    Furnished homes, a 28-night minimum stay and a target cap of $9,500 per 28 nights.

  8. 08

    Hold & exit

    24–36 months

    From purchase to sale, after renovation and a period of rental operation.

  9. 09

    Deal size

    $750K–$2M

    All-in cost per home.

  10. 10

    Minimum return

    15% investor IRR

    The deck’s screening hurdle, after carry and gains tax.

These are selection targets, subject to property-level underwriting and due diligence. They do not establish that every condition is already satisfied or guarantee a return.

What full due diligence covers

Structure, roof, waterproofing, plumbing, electrical systems, humidity, site and soil; permits and licensing; title and liens; HOA rental rules and pending assessments. A works budget with contingency is required before commitment.

Targets and the current model

The deck’s 15% screening hurdle measures investor-level IRR after carry and gains tax. Model 07 separately targets 20% deal-level IRR after gains tax. These are different return measures. The fund’s projected 15.7% investor IRR is a model output, distinct from either screening target.

The nightly range and deal-size range are screening targets. Rental rates and budgets are underwritten property by property and may fall outside these ranges.

The entry discount compares all-in cost with improved comparable value. Each acquisition requires full technical, legal and title review.

Enjoyment is a property-selection objective. Any investor use rights, allocation or access are governed by the Membership documents.
